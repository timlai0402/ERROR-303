Operational SME Journey
Mechanic workshop: Oil filter replenishment workflow (Trigger → Connected Data → Decision Logic → Human Approval → Visible Outcome → Scale)
1

1. Trigger — Workshop Consumption

Mechanic consumes 8 units of Oil Filter (FLT-02) for morning car servicing, dropping stock from 16 to 8.

2

2. Connected Data — Automatic Linkage

System links SKU FLT-02, current stock (8 pcs), min threshold (20 pcs), and supplier Huat Kee Auto Spares (+6012-8889999).

3

3. Decision Logic — Deterministic Deficit Calculation

System computes deficit of exactly 12 units (20 − 8) and triggers an in-app Low Stock alert.

4

4. Human Approval — Restock Sign-off

Shop owner approves incoming supplier delivery and commits a 12-unit Stock In replenishment.

5

5. Visible Outcome — Atomic Update

Stock balance restores to 20 pcs atomically, low-stock alert clears, and movement is saved in immutable ledger.

6

6. Scale — Independent SME Catalog

Shop scales catalog to 1,000+ non-expiry hardware items with batch CSV validation and multi-tenant isolation.
